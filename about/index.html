Hello there, I'm Judah 👋

I'm an Orange County-based photographer with 2+ years of experience capturing events and the unique moments that make them memorable. What started with picking up an old camera has grown into a passion for capturing genuine, authentic moments. I try to stay unobtrusive and let things happen naturally, while always looking for unique and creative shots that tell the story of what's happening.

When I'm not behind the camera, you'll usually find me swimming at the beach or editing photos at San Clemente's local coffee shops. Photography has become a big part of my life, and I love getting to meet new people, experience different events, and find moments that might otherwise go unnoticed.

If you have an event you'd like captured, I'd love to be there. Whether it's a concert, sporting event, or community event, I'll be there to capture the genuine moments as they happen. Reach out to book a shoot or to talk about what you're looking for. I'd love to hear about your event and see how I can help tell its story through photos.
